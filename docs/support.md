# Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/drew-bomar/dodo/issues).

## Common questions

**My tasks aren't on the Lock Screen.**
Open DoDo, tap the gear, and turn on **Show on Lock Screen**. Also check that Live Activities are allowed for the app in **Settings → Apps → DoDo → Live Activities**.

**The card disappeared in the evening.**
iOS keeps a Lock Screen card active for up to 8 hours, then removes it within 4 more. A new card starts at noon each day, or as soon as you open DoDo.

**I swiped the card away. How do I get it back?**
Open DoDo, tap the gear, and tap **Show Today's Card Again**.

**How do I move my tasks to a new iPhone?**
They move with your iPhone backup. You can also tap the gear, then **Export Backup…**, and on the other iPhone use **Restore from Backup…**.
