# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 1, 2026_

DoDo is a to-do list app. It doesn't collect, share or sell any information about you.

## What stays on your iPhone

Your tasks, goals and history are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. They are included in your iPhone's backups (iCloud or computer) if you have those turned on, under your own Apple account. The app has no servers and no accounts. It sends nothing over the internet.

## Lock Screen

If you turn on **Show on Lock Screen**, the titles of your next tasks appear on your Lock Screen, where anyone holding your iPhone can see them, even while it's locked. This is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in the app's settings.

## Backups you export

**Export Backup** creates a file containing your tasks and history and saves it wherever you choose, such as iCloud Drive. That file is yours. The app doesn't upload it anywhere.

## Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports to the developer. Apple's privacy policy covers those.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/drew-bomar/dodo/issues).
